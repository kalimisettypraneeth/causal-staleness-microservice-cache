## Evidence discipline

- Peer-reviewed systems/database papers define scholarly overlap; prototypes are linked only when verified.
- Preprints and extended reports are labeled separately from peer-reviewed records.
- Historical work is included when it directly constrains “cost-aware,” “selective validation,” or compensation claims.
- Trace-context propagation, cross-tier causal correlation, rare-event capture, and general retry/work amplification are treated as established mechanisms, not as the cache contribution.
- Broad graph-aware, dependency-aware, bounded-staleness, causal-cache, inconsistency-cost, and cost-triggered-refresh novelty is rejected.
- The proposed physical-unit downstream-work attribution and resulting policy remain **UNVERIFIED** pending stale-state-specific attribution, direct inconsistent-state retry/compensation studies, alternative cost-aware freshness terminology, artifact compatibility, and citation-chain searches.
